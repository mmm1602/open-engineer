##  What  is fluid mechanics?
  
Subset branch of physics, governed by physical laws.
Fluid mechanics:

The science that deals with fluids at rest and the interaction of fluids with solids or other fluids at the boundaries.

Fluid Dynamics: Fluid mechanics is also referred to as fluid dynamics by considering fluids at rest as a special case of motion with zero velocity.
  

First exam only deals with static fluids.

Hydrodynamics: Approximated incompressible fluid
Hydraulics: liquid flow
Gas Dynamics
Aerodynamics

Meteorology, oceanography, and hydrology.
