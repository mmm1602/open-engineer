# Fluid Mechanics: Fundamentals

> [!info] Core Definition
> **Fluid Mechanics** is the branch of physics and engineering science that deals with fluids (liquids and gases) at rest or in motion, as well as the interactions between fluids and solids or other fluids at their boundaries.

---

## 1. What is a Fluid?

A **fluid** is a substance in either the **liquid** or **gas** phase. 

### Solid vs. Fluid Behavior Under Shear Stress
* **Solid:** Can resist an applied shear stress by deforming. Static deformation is proportional to stress.
* **Fluid:** Deforms **continuously** under the influence of a shear stress, no matter how small that stress is.

> [!note] Definition: Stress
> $$\text{Stress} = \frac{\text{Force}}{\text{Unit Area}}$$

### Primary Classifications

- **Fluid Dynamics:** Covers fluids in motion. (Note: Fluid mechanics is often broadly referred to as _fluid dynamics_ by treating fluids at rest as a special case where velocity = 0).
    
- **Fluid Statics:** Deals exclusively with fluids at rest.
    

> [!exam] Course Scope **Exam 1 Focus:** Covers **static fluids** (fluid statics) only!

### Specialized Disciplines

- **Hydrodynamics:** The study of fluid motion where the fluid is approximated as **incompressible** (e.g., water under low speeds).
    
- **Hydraulics:** The practical engineering branch dealing with **liquid flow** in pipes, open channels, and hydraulic machinery.
    
- **Gas Dynamics:** The study of fluids that undergo significant density changes (compressible gas flows at high speeds).
    
- **Aerodynamics:** The study of air flow over solid bodies (aircraft, vehicles, structures).
    

## 3. Interdisciplinary Applications

Fluid mechanics forms the foundational physics for major environmental and physical sciences:

- **Meteorology:** Atmospheric fluid dynamics.
    
- **Oceanography:** Ocean currents and wave mechanics.
    
- **Hydrology:** Water movement on and under Earth's surface.
